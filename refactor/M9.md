# M9 — Dead-Code & Backcompat-Shim Sweep

**Depends on:** none. **Unlocks:** cleaner diffs for M12–M16.
**Dimensions:** 12 (dead code), 13 (consistency). **Priority:** high. **Size:** S.
**Risk:** low (pure deletion).

## Objective

Delete code that exists only to serve its own test or to bridge a long-completed
move. ~400 LOC removed, zero behavior change.

## Why

The repo's alpha stance forbids backcompat shims (`CLAUDE.md` Stability), yet
several survive, and a few helpers are kept alive solely by their tests. Each is
a pure deletion that shrinks surface and maintenance drag. Confirmed by the
foundation, runtime, prompt, and both adapter reviewers.

## Scope (each item independent)

- **`Tool.wrap` + `_resolve_wrapped_*`** — `prompt/tool.py:579-691` (~110 LOC); only
  caller is its own test. `Tool[...].create()` is the sole constructor. Delete fn,
  helpers, test.
- **`safe_hook_wrapper`** — `adapters/claude_agent_sdk/_hooks.py:584-668` (84 LOC,
  in `__all__`); the SDK runs hooks via `build_hooks_config`; only its own test
  calls it (uses deprecated `get_event_loop().run_until_complete` + string-keyed
  exception matching). Delete fn, export, test.
- **`session/dataclasses.py`** — pure re-export of `is_dataclass_instance`; repoint
  `runtime/session/snapshots.py:31` and `session_telemetry.py:32` to
  `weakincentives.types.dataclass`; delete the module.
- **`ACPSessionState` + accumulator** — `adapters/acp/_state.py`, exported from
  acp/opencode_acp/gemini_acp `__init__`; never dispatched. Plus the dead
  `client.set_accumulator`/`_accumulator` branch (`acp/client.py:67,103-104`).
  Delete all (spec edit in M11).
- **`_async.py` re-export stubs** — `adapters/acp/_async.py`,
  `adapters/codex_app_server/_async.py`; repoint importers to `.._shared`; delete.
- **Duplicate `Snapshotable`** — `runtime/snapshotable.py` duplicates
  `resources/protocols.py:100` (~68 LOC); the runtime copy is the one used by
  `isinstance` in `transactions.py`. Re-export from `resources`; delete the
  runtime module.

## Exit criteria

- `grep -rn "Tool\.wrap\|safe_hook_wrapper\|ACPSessionState" src/` is empty;
  `session/dataclasses.py` and the two `_async.py` stubs are gone; one
  `Snapshotable` remains.
- `make check` green.
