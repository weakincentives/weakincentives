# M5 — Definition Composition & Isolation Testing

**Core:** Definition. **Release:** R2. **Depends on:** R1 (definitions carry a
`SandboxConfig`; tests fake a `Sandbox`). **Unlocks:** [M6](M6.md). **Size:** M–L.
**Risk:** low–med.

## Objective

Definitions become **composable from reusable, harness-independent units** —
with that independence checker-enforced — and **unit-testable with no real
harness** via a first-class `FakeHarness` and a public testing surface.

## Why (first principles)

*The definition is code; pure core means testable core.* `CLAUDE.md` already
promises "version, review, test, and port," and `prompt/` already imports no
adapter — but the independence is incidental (unenforced) and the testing story
is ad-hoc: `grep "class .*Adapter" tests/` finds a dozen one-off mock adapters,
and the flagship `code_reviewer_example.py` (~20k LOC) builds its sections with
private local helpers (`_create_workspace_section`,
`PromptTemplate[ReviewResponse].create(...)`) that nothing can reuse. Definitions
are the thing users *write*; composing and testing them must be the paved road.
This is also where the typed-composition debt lives: **three** parallel
generic-specialization mechanisms (`Tool.__class_getitem__` at
`tool.py:228-253,542-577`; `PromptTemplate.__class_getitem__` at
`prompt.py:117-140`; `GenericParamsSpecializer` used only by `Section`) drive the
layer's worst cast cluster.

## Stages (each lands green)

1. **Packs** — a documented pattern plus light helpers for reusable
   section/tool/policy packs: a pack is a function returning a `Section`,
   `tuple[Tool, …]`, or `tuple[Policy, …]` that any definition drops in. Refactor
   `code_reviewer_example.py`'s builders into the canonical example. The paved
   road includes a deliberate import surface: `prompt/__init__.py` exports 75
   symbols in one flat namespace (`__init__.py:346`), mixing the core
   surface (`PromptTemplate`, `Section`, `Tool`) with overrides subclasses and
   validator internals — curate it into tiers (core / extension / internal) so
   packs compose against an intentional API.
1. **One specialization mechanism** — a single `GenericArgCapture` consumed by
   `Tool`, `PromptTemplate`, and `Section`; delete the other two mechanisms and
   their cast clusters.
1. **Boundary checker** — extend `toolchain/checkers`: a definition module (the
   `prompt/` surface and user definition packages) importing any `adapters/*`
   symbol fails `make check`. Independence becomes an invariant, not a habit.
1. **`FakeHarness`** — a deterministic `ProviderAdapter` driven by a scripted
   plan (text / tool-call / structured-output steps); no network, no provider,
   honors the tool-bridging and event contracts so definition tests exercise the
   real dispatch path.
1. **`weakincentives.testing`** — public helpers: *render & assert* (prompt text,
   tool schemas, enabled sections, policies, output type — golden-friendly);
   *drive a tool* (fabricated `ToolContext` over a `LocalSandbox`/`MemoryBackend`
   with `FakeClock`; assert `ToolResult`, session/sandbox effects, rollback);
   *exercise policies/feedback/completion* directly. Replace the ad-hoc mock
   adapters across `tests/`; document the pattern in `specs/TESTING.md`.

## Exit criteria

- A representative definition (the code-review example) is assembled from packs
  and fully unit-tested — render goldens, tool drives, policy checks — with no
  provider, under the 10 s/test budget.
- One specialization mechanism remains; the boundary checker fails on a
  definition→adapter import.
- `make check` green.
