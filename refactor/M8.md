# M8 — Policies as the One Control Plane

**Core:** Definition + Contract. **Release:** R3. **Depends on:** R1 (egress),
[M7](M7.md) (decisions land in evidence). **Size:** M–L. **Risk:** med.

## Objective

One declarative, composable, fail-closed, **observable** policy engine governing
tool gating, egress, budgets, and task completion — replacing four scattered
mechanisms with one concept.

## Why (first principles)

*Policies over workflows is the philosophy; make it the architecture — and the
durable steering surface.* As harnesses absorb planning, sub-agents, and memory
over the next releases of the ecosystem, prescriptive control evaporates;
declarative constraints are what remain yours. Today enforcement is fragmented:
tool policies (`SequentialDependencyPolicy`, `ReadBeforeWritePolicy` in
`prompt/policy.py`), the egress allowlist (R1, control-plane), budget checkpoints
(`budget.py` + adapter checks), and the task-completion checker
(`prompt/task_completion.py`) are four shapes with four wiring paths. A policy is
one thing: a pure decision function at a decision point.

## Stages (each lands green)

1. **The engine** — `Policy` protocol evaluated at typed decision points
   (`pre_tool`, `egress_change`, `credential_change`, `budget_checkpoint`,
   `completion`), returning a structured `Decision` (allow / deny + reason /
   request-feedback). Conjunction composition; deterministic ordering;
   **fail-closed** on any deny or error.
1. **Decisions are evidence** — every evaluation dispatches a decision event
   (visible in session, transcript, bundle, and `ReplayTrace`), with the policy
   id, inputs digest, and outcome. "Why was this blocked?" becomes a query.
1. **Port and delete** — reimplement the existing guardrails on the engine with
   identical behavior (sequential-dependency, read-before-write, egress, budget,
   completion) and delete the bespoke paths, including the per-adapter
   completion-gating glue consolidated in M6.

## Exit criteria

- All four guardrail families run on one engine; behavior is unchanged
  (existing guardrail tests pass re-targeted); every decision is an event.
- An agent cannot widen its own egress or rebind credentials:
  `egress_change`/`credential_change` decisions originate only from
  control-plane callers, and decision events never carry secret material.
- `make check` green.
