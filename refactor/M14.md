# M14 — De-Leak Runtime Decomposition

**Depends on:** none. **Dimensions:** 1, 2, 4. **Priority:** med–high. **Size:** M.
**Risk:** med.

## Objective

Make the `session/` and `agent_loop` subsystems genuinely encapsulated. Today they
were split for line-count but the helpers reach back into owner privates through
shims and a private-mirror protocol.

## Why

- `session.py:602-644` is a "Backward Compatibility Attributes" block forwarding
  into `SliceStore`/`ReducerRegistry` privates with
  `# pyright: ignore[reportPrivateUsage]` (lines 614, 625, 633, 644); **6** session
  modules still carry file-level `reportPrivateUsage` waivers.
- `_agent_loop_bundle.py:57` declares a `_LoopLike` Protocol mirroring 7 private
  `AgentLoop` members, needing a `self`-cast at the call site; `_reply_and_ack`
  (`agent_loop.py`) is documented as a "test shim" but is production.

The "thin facade + subsystems" design in `SESSIONS.md` is partly fictional while
these escapes exist; the blanket waivers also hide future private-access mistakes.

## Scope

- Give `SliceStore`/`ReducerRegistry` the small public methods their callers need
  (`snapshot_registrations`/`apply_registrations`, `iter_slices`/`replace_slice`);
  **delete** the `session.py:602-644` backcompat block and the file-level
  `reportPrivateUsage` waivers.
- Make the bundle helper take public collaborators (adapter, config, callables)
  instead of the whole loop; **delete** `_LoopLike` and its `self`-cast.
- Fix/remove the `_reply_and_ack` shim and its docstring (callers can use the
  module-level `reply_and_ack`).

## Exit criteria

- No private-mirror protocols; the session backcompat block and its suppressions
  are gone; `grep -rn "reportPrivateUsage" src/weakincentives/runtime/session/`
  is empty.
- `make check` green.
