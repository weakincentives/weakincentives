# M10 — Test Definitions in Isolation

**Release:** R2. **Depends on:** [M9](M9.md), R1 (fake `Sandbox`). **Unlocks:**
[M12](M12.md). **Dimensions:** 10, 13. **Priority:** high (mission, the headline).
**Size:** M.

## Objective

Make it first-class to **unit-test an agent definition with no real harness** —
render it and assert its shape, and drive its tools and policies directly — so
"testable in isolation from the underlying harness" is real, fast, and standard.

## Why (first principles)

*The definition is code you test in isolation.* Today there is no shared way to do
this: test-time adapters are **ad-hoc per file** (`grep "class .*Adapter" tests/`
returns a dozen one-off mocks), and definition authors re-invent the scaffolding
each time. The pieces exist — `FakeClock`, a constructible `ToolContext`, and (from
R1) a `LocalSandbox`/in-memory backend — but they don't compose into a "test your
definition" surface. Definitions are the thing users *write*; testing them must be
the easy path.

## Scope

- **`FakeHarness`** — a deterministic `ProviderAdapter` driven by a scripted
  response plan (ordered text / tool-call / structured-output steps); no network,
  no provider, sub-10 s.
- **Assertion helpers** (a public `weakincentives.testing` surface):
  - *render & assert* — render a definition and assert prompt text, tool schemas,
    enabled sections, policies, and the structured-output type (golden tests);
  - *drive a tool* — invoke a handler with a fabricated `ToolContext` over a
    `LocalSandbox` + `FakeClock`, asserting `ToolResult` and session/sandbox
    effects (incl. rollback);
  - *exercise policies/feedback/task-completion* directly.
- **Adopt it** — replace the ad-hoc per-test mock adapters with `FakeHarness`;
  document the pattern in `specs/TESTING.md`.

## Exit criteria

- A representative definition, its tools, and its policies are unit-tested with no
  provider, via `FakeHarness` + helpers; the ad-hoc mock adapters are gone.
- `make check` green (suite stays within the 10 s/test budget).
