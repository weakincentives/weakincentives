# M12 — Runtime De-Leak & Encapsulation

**Release:** R2. **Depends on:** none. **Unlocks:** cleaner serialization (M9) and
conformance (M11). **Dimensions:** 1, 2, 4. **Priority:** med–high. **Size:** M.
**Risk:** med.

## Objective

Make the `session/` and `agent_loop` subsystems genuinely encapsulated. They were
split for line-count but the helpers reach back into owner privates through shims
and a private-mirror protocol — which blocks clean serialization and a trustworthy
conformance gate.

## Why (first principles)

A definition/run you want to serialize and conformance-test must have real
boundaries. Today `session.py:602-644` is a "Backward Compatibility Attributes"
block forwarding into `SliceStore`/`ReducerRegistry` privates with
`# pyright: ignore[reportPrivateUsage]` (lines 614, 625, 633, 644); **6** session
modules carry file-level `reportPrivateUsage` waivers; `_agent_loop_bundle.py:57`
declares a `_LoopLike` Protocol mirroring 7 private `AgentLoop` members and needs a
`self`-cast at the call site. The "thin facade + subsystems" design in
`SESSIONS.md` is partly fictional while these escapes exist.

## Scope

- Give `SliceStore`/`ReducerRegistry` the small public methods their callers need
  (`snapshot_registrations`/`apply_registrations`, `iter_slices`/`replace_slice`);
  **delete** the `session.py:602-644` block and the file-level waivers.
- Make the bundle helper take public collaborators instead of the whole loop;
  **delete** `_LoopLike` and its `self`-cast.
- Fix/remove the `_reply_and_ack` "test shim" (it is production).

## Exit criteria

- `grep -rn "reportPrivateUsage" src/weakincentives/runtime/session/` is empty; no
  private-mirror protocols remain.
- `make check` green.
